Khoya Machine Buying Guide in India

Choosing the right khoya machine depends on production capacity, construction quality, heating requirements and the type of dairy products you plan to manufacture.

Check Production Capacity

Before selecting a machine, estimate the amount of milk you need to process each day. The required capacity can vary depending on the scale of the dairy operation.

Consider Machine Construction

Food processing equipment should be easy to clean and suitable for regular dairy production.

Compare Heating Options

Different khoya machines may use gas, electricity, steam or other heating systems. The appropriate option depends on your production setup and operating requirements.

Maintenance Matters

Regular cleaning and maintenance can help keep dairy processing equipment working efficiently.
