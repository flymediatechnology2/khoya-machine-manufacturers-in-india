Being an owner of a dairy business means the necessity of having enough information regarding choosing the right type of khoya making machine. Your entire output and productivity depend on how well the khoya making machines have been operated and how much capacity they have been producing.

Apparently, for small- or large-scale dairy businesses, choosing the right type of khoya making machine entirely depends on the specific volume in demand. Choosing a khoya making machine with an extra 20% - 30% capacity for producing khoya can be a reliable choice for your business size. Welcome to this blog post where you’ll be introduced to the specific points explaining how to choose the right khoya making machine, including practical scenarios for better understanding.

First, understand why the right size of a khoya making machine matters for your dairy business!

  • Khoya making machines basically include some semi-automatic or fully automatic models; therefore, if you choose the right one according to your business requirements, productivity will increase.
  • Choosing the right size khoya making machine can prevent the utility bills from rising, as large machines for small businesses and small sizes for large businesses can waste power.
  • The ingredients used for the khoya-making process will be prevented from being wasted, ensuring proper preservation of your ingredients.

Choosing the right size for a small dairy business!

  • The small dairy business requirement for khoya production is quite low, around 10-25 kg per day; therefore, choosing a small semi-automatic khoya machine would be the best choice.
  • Make sure to analyze the machine’s daily processing limit thoroughly to help meet your specific demands and avoid overprocessing and exhausting multiple batches.

Choosing the right size for a medium dairy business!

  • Since the requirement for a medium-sized dairy business is higher than a small one, which is 30 to 50 kg per day, choosing a medium-sized semi-automatic khoya machine would be the best choice.
  • Make sure to avoid the use of electric-only heating systems for this size of dairy business, as it might result in high running costs.
  • Choosing an LPG gas forced or steam-jacketed system would be the reliable choice for this size.

Choosing the right size for a large-sized dairy business!

  • The real selection process starts here, when you need a khoya making machine for your large-sized dairy business.
  • Since it is large, the requirements for khoya production would be more than 50 kg; hence, choosing a fully automatic khoya machine would work well.
  • Calculate your daily demand, as this can fluctuate some days; after this, make sure to analyze your machine’s processing limit in a single day and then proceed further.

Other key factors to analyze when choosing a khoya making machine!

  • Analyse the heating control of a khoya-making machine, as it plays a crucial role in removing moisture from milk to maintain consistency.
  • Ensure you evaluate the right material, including stainless steel, which is quite easy to clean.

The Bottom Line!

Summing up the above-mentioned points, you might have understood the specific guide for choosing the right size of khoya making machine according to your dairy business size and capacity needs.
